Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

English · 中文

About this policy

HSK Words (New HSK Vocabulary Trainer) is developed and operated by Yunhao Zhong. This policy describes its current free vocabulary practice features. You can use the app without creating an account.

Learning data stays on your device

The app stores your answers, progress, mistake history, saved words, study scope, preferences and any existing Plus entitlement status in a local database. These records support practice and remember your settings. We do not upload your learning records to a developer-operated server or provide an app account or cloud learning sync service.

Advertising and analytics

The current app contains no advertising, advertising tracking or developer-integrated third-party analytics service. It does not request access to your contacts, photos, microphone or location for vocabulary practice. We do not sell your learning data.

Apple services and connectivity

The app can ask Apple's App Store services about existing purchase entitlements at startup, even though new Plus purchase entry points are currently hidden. Purchase information is processed on the device to retain an existing entitlement; we do not operate a receipt-validation server or receive your payment card details. Apple handles its services under its own privacy policy. App Store or TestFlight diagnostics and feedback may be available to us through Apple according to your settings and actions. Basic vocabulary practice uses the embedded word library. Remote vocabulary downloads are not enabled in this release.

Retention, deletion and backups

Local learning data remains until you remove it. Reset progress on Home clears answer and review history for the selected study scope, including its mistake progress; it does not remove saved words or preferences. Remove saved words individually in Favorites. To remove all of the app's local data on iOS, choose Delete App, not Offload App. Device backups may retain or restore app data; manage those separately in your Apple backup settings. We cannot access or remotely delete your local learning database.

Contacting support

If you email hskwords@foxmail.com, we receive your email address and the message or attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate problems and handle privacy requests, not for marketing. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store it. Please avoid sending sensitive information or identifiable student information that is not needed. We retain correspondence only as needed to handle the request and related follow-up or legal obligations; you may request deletion by email.

This website

These support and privacy pages are hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security, including visits without a GitHub account. We do not add analytics, advertising scripts or cookies to these pages. GitHub handles hosting data under its Privacy Statement, including its retention practices. Opening this website is separate from storing learning records inside the app.

Children and students

Vocabulary practice does not require students to submit personal information or create accounts. A parent, guardian or teacher can contact us about privacy concerns. Please do not send unnecessary identifying information about a child.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy when data practices change and show the updated date. For privacy questions or requests, contact HSK Words support at hskwords@foxmail.com.

中文

关于本政策

HSK Words（New HSK Vocabulary Trainer）由 Yunhao Zhong 开发和运营。本政策说明当前免费词汇练习功能的数据处理方式。使用 App 无需注册账号。

学习数据保存在你的设备上

App 在本地数据库中保存答题记录、学习进度、错题历史、收藏、学习范围、偏好设置以及可能已有的 Plus 权益状态，用于练习和记住设置。我们不会将学习记录上传到开发者运营的服务器，也没有 App 账号或学习记录云端同步服务。

广告与统计

当前 App 不含广告、广告追踪或开发者接入的第三方行为统计服务。词汇练习不请求访问通讯录、照片、麦克风或位置。我们不会出售你的学习数据。

Apple 服务与网络连接

即使当前隐藏了新购买 Plus 的入口，App 启动时仍可能通过 Apple App Store 服务检查已有购买权益。购买信息在设备上用于保留已有权益；我们没有运营收据验证服务器，也不会收到你的银行卡信息。Apple 按其自身隐私政策处理相关服务。根据你的设置和主动操作，Apple 可能向我们提供 App Store 或 TestFlight 的诊断信息和反馈。基础词汇练习使用内置词库，当前版本未启用远程词库下载。

保留、删除与备份

本地学习数据会保留到你移除它。首页的 Reset progress 会清除当前学习范围内的答题和复习历史，包括错题进度，但不会清除收藏或偏好设置。收藏可在 Favorites 中逐条移除。要清除 App 的全部本地数据，请在 iOS 中选择“删除 App”，而不是会保留数据的“卸载 App”。设备备份可能保留或恢复 App 数据，请在 Apple 备份设置中单独管理。我们无法访问或远程删除你的本地学习数据库。

联系支持

如果你向 hskwords@foxmail.com 发邮件，我们会收到你的邮箱地址，以及你主动提供的正文或附件，仅用于回复、排查问题和处理隐私请求，不用于营销。邮件服务提供商会为投递和存储邮件处理这些通信。请不要发送非必要的敏感信息或可识别学生身份的信息。邮件仅在处理请求、相关后续事项或履行法律义务所需期间保留；你可以通过邮件申请删除。

本网站

本支持页面和隐私政策页面由 GitHub Pages 托管。GitHub 会出于安全目的记录访问者的 IP 地址，包括未登录 GitHub 的访问。我们未在页面中添加统计、广告脚本或 Cookie。托管数据的处理和保留方式遵循 GitHub 隐私声明。访问本网站与 App 内本地保存学习记录是不同的数据处理场景。

儿童与学生

词汇练习不要求学生提供个人信息或注册账号。家长、监护人或教师可以就隐私问题联系我们，请避免发送非必要的儿童身份信息。

更新与联系方式

如果数据处理方式发生变化，我们会更新本政策并注明更新日期。如有隐私问题或请求，请通过 hskwords@foxmail.com 联系 HSK Words 支持。
