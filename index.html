Support / 支持

English · 中文

Contact

Email chineseissohard@foxmail.com for help, vocabulary corrections or privacy requests. You do not need an account to ask for help.

Report a problem

Please include the app version shown in Settings, your device and iOS version, what you did and what happened. A screenshot can help, but remove personal information first. Never send passwords or payment details.

Suggest a vocabulary correction

Send the Chinese word, its HSK level, the meaning shown in the app and your suggested correction. A reliable reference is helpful. You do not need to share your learning history.

Progress and saved words

Learning records are stored locally. Reset progress affects the current study scope and does not delete Favorites. Favorites can be removed individually. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading keeps it. Device backups are managed separately by Apple. We do not offer account-based recovery or sync.

Mistake Book

A word graduates after the configured number of consecutive correct Mistake Practice answers. The default is 3; change it from 1 to 999 in Settings. An incorrect answer resets the streak. Graduation does not remove the word from Favorites.

Free vocabulary

All current HSK 1-9 word lists and Exam practice are free. HSK 7-9 is one combined level. No purchase is required for the current vocabulary library. New Plus purchases are not offered in this version.

Content sources and limitations

Learning materials may contain errors or omissions; completeness and accuracy are not guaranteed. Read our Content Sources & Disclaimer for sources, independent-app status and feedback details.

中文

联系我们

如需帮助、反馈词条错误或提出隐私请求，请发邮件至 chineseissohard@foxmail.com。联系支持无需注册账号。

反馈问题

请说明 Settings 中显示的 App 版本、设备型号、iOS 版本、操作步骤和实际结果。可以附截图，但请先遮挡个人信息。不要发送密码或支付资料。

反馈词条错误

请提供汉字、HSK 等级、App 中显示的释义和建议修改内容，有可靠参考来源更好。无需发送学习历史。

进度与收藏

学习记录保存在本机。Reset progress 只影响当前学习范围，不会删除 Favorites。收藏可逐条移除。“删除 App”会移除本地数据，“卸载 App”则会保留。设备备份由 Apple 单独管理；我们不提供基于账号的恢复或同步服务。

错题本毕业

词条需要在 Mistake Practice 中连续答对指定次数才会毕业。默认 3 次，可在 Settings 调整为 1–999 次。答错会重置连续答对次数，毕业不会自动删除收藏。

免费词库

当前全部 HSK 1–9 词表和 Exam 练习免费。HSK 7–9 保持为一个合并等级，使用现有词库无需购买。本版本不提供新购买 Plus 的入口。

内容来源与使用限制

学习资料可能存在错误或遗漏，不保证完整性或准确性。资料来源、独立开发声明及反馈方式见内容来源与说明。
