Independent learning app

中文好难: Chinese Is So Hard is an independently developed vocabulary learning app. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Chinese Testing International or the organizations responsible for the HSK examination. HSK names and level labels identify the learning scope; they do not imply official status or approval.

Vocabulary sources

The vocabulary selection and level assignments reference the New HSK Examination Syllabus (新版HSK考试大纲, PDF file marked 1219) linked by the Chinese Test Service Website. See the source syllabus and the website's legal notice. HSK 7-9 is presented as a combined level.

English meanings are AI-assisted and reviewed and edited by the developer, a Chinese-language teacher. They are learning aids, not official HSK translations. The app's vocabulary questions are not official HSK examination questions or a complete simulation of the exam. The app icon and background artwork were created using AI tools.

We work to review and improve the materials, but they may contain errors or omissions. We do not guarantee their completeness or accuracy. Meanings can vary with context, and concise definitions do not cover every usage. For current examination requirements, consult the official examination organizer. Using this app does not guarantee an examination result.
